# MedRisk Audit Lab — Final Evaluation Report (v1.0)

> Research, education, and portfolio demonstration only. Not a medical
> device. Data: US hospitals 1999–2008; results do not generalize to
> current practice or to present-day Japanese healthcare.

## 1. What was evaluated

A LightGBM classifier predicting 30-day hospital readmission
(`readmitted == "<30"` positive) on the public UCI "Diabetes 130-US
Hospitals" dataset, audited end to end: patient-level splits, one-shot
final-test isolation, calibration, threshold analysis, subgroup audit,
abstention, and a constrained LLM explanation layer evaluated
quantitatively.

- Cohort: 101,766 encounters → 2,423 death/hospice exclusions → 99,343
  encounters, 69,990 patients, 11.4% positive.
- Splits by `patient_nbr` (seed 20260717, hash `da093bcc…`):
  train 41,994 / calibration 6,999 / validation 10,498 / test 10,499
  patients. Non-overlap is CI-tested.
- Frozen before final test: LGBM (num_leaves 31, lr 0.05, 200 trees,
  class-balanced), Platt calibration, threshold 0.16 (max validation F1),
  abstention band ±0.015.

## 2. Final-test results (executed once; n=15,076 encounters)

| Metric | Value | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| AUROC | 0.688 | [0.674, 0.702] |
| AUPRC (prevalence 0.117) | 0.244 | [0.224, 0.266] |
| Brier score | 0.098 | [0.093, 0.102] |
| Recall | 0.384 | [0.358, 0.412] |
| Specificity | 0.844 | [0.836, 0.852] |
| Precision | 0.245 | [0.228, 0.263] |
| F1 | 0.299 | [0.279, 0.320] |
| ECE | 0.009 | [0.005, 0.015] |

Validation → final-test AUROC (0.677 → 0.688) shows no selection overfit.
Confusion matrix and reliability data: `final_test_metrics.json`;
figures: `final_roc_pr.png`, `final_reliability.png`.

## 3. Calibration

Platt scaling (fitted on the calibration split only) reduced validation
ECE from 0.321 to 0.007; final-test ECE is 0.009 with slope 1.257 and
intercept 0.516 — probabilities are usable as probabilities, which is the
point of a risk model.

## 4. Subgroup audit (final test)

AUROC is statistically indistinguishable across the large race and gender
groups; every entry pairs the metric with group size and CI, and groups
under 50 patients are labeled "insufficient sample for conclusions"
(`final_test_subgroups.json`). This is an observational audit, not a
fairness guarantee (see `docs/ETHICS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`).

## 5. Abstention

At the frozen band the system answers 89.8% of final-test cases and
improves selective F1 to 0.306 (vs 0.299 answering everything) — a small,
honest gain that demonstrates the mechanism.

## 6. Error analysis

The ten most confident validation misclassifications with their SHAP
attributions are published in `reports/explain/error_analysis.json`.
Recurrent pattern: high prior-inpatient-visit counts dominate confident
false positives; the model has no post-discharge context to distinguish
successfully stabilized patients.

## 7. LLM explanation evaluation (no live API was called)

104 versioned cases (validation-derived + synthetic edge/corrupted), three
conditions, eight dimensions, outputs generated by a deterministic
StubClient and labeled as such. Violation rates:

| Dimension | LLM-only | +model results | full contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| Numeric alteration | 0.99 | 0.10 | 0.00 |
| Fabricated features | 1.00 | 0.10 | 0.00 |
| Unsupported medical assertions | 1.00 | 0.10 | 0.00 |
| Critical-information omission | 1.00 | 0.22 | 0.00 |
| Abstention compliance | 0.15 | 0.24 | 0.00 |
| Structured-output failure | 0.00 | 0.10 | 0.00 |
| Consistency failure | 0.00 | 0.10 | 0.00 |

The gradient is the message: an unconstrained "LLM as predictor" fails
almost every check; the full contract passes all of them. Failure examples
for every dimension: `reports/llm/failure_examples.json`. Human-rubric
scoring (`reports/llm/human_rubric_template.csv`) is prepared but not yet
scored — it requires a human and is listed as an open item.

## 8. Reproducibility

Checksummed data acquisition, versioned split lists, seeded determinism
(same seed ⇒ identical split hash, LGBM predictions, and metrics — test-
enforced), pinned `uv.lock`, and a run manifest per stage recording data,
config, split hashes, package versions, and git commit.

## 9. Honest summary

Moderate discrimination (AUROC 0.69) consistent with published results on
this dataset; excellent calibration after Platt scaling; a working,
measured abstention mechanism; a subgroup audit that refuses to
over-claim; and an LLM layer whose failure modes are measured and
published rather than hidden. The contribution is the audit, not the
score.
